Forty-five years of drawing, engineering and building the rooms people gather in.

Sir William Designs is a family business specializing in high-end millwork. Every project begins at the drawing board: we design each piece around your space and engineer it down to the joinery, panels and hardware.

The result is cabinetry that looks effortless on the first day and still works beautifully decades later.

What we make

We work directly with homeowners and alongside architects, interior designers and builders, from a single vanity to every room in the house.

Kitchens

Full kitchens, islands and appliance walls with integrated panels, from first sketch to final hardware.

Bars and pantries

Wet bars, sculleries and butler's pantries with wine storage, glass uppers and stone niches.

Built-ins and media walls

Libraries, media walls and display shelving, with lighting and wiring planned into the design.

Baths and dressing rooms

Floating vanities, linen storage and custom closets built to the same standard as the kitchen.

From drawing to installation

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We visit the space, listen to how you live and review your plans or inspiration.

  2. 2

    Design

    Drawings and renderings that settle layout, proportions, materials and finishes.

  3. 3

    Engineering

    Shop drawings for every panel, joint and fitting, coordinated with your builder and trades.

  4. 4

    Fabrication

    Built to the drawings with close attention to grain matching, reveals and finish.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Installed, scribed and adjusted on site until every door and drawer is right.
